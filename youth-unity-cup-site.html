Youth Unity Cup

Rules & Regulations

A summary of the rules. Full regulations will be published before the draw.

Eligibility

  • Players must be under 19 and verified with a live photo and approved ID.
  • Each squad has up to 18 players and 3 team officials.
  • Players complete pre-competition health screening.

Format

  • 20 teams in 5 groups of 4, round-robin, 30 group matches.
  • Quarter-finals: 5 group winners and the 3 best runners-up.
  • Then semi-finals, a 3rd place match and the Final.

Match days and spectators

  • Matches are played on Saturdays and Sundays across 8 weekends.
  • Entry is free. Each venue is capped at 700 spectators.
  • No cash is handled by community leadership; all payments go through the audited account.
Youth Unity Cup

About the Youth Unity Cup

A community-owned football platform that tests, verifies and prepares LGAs for lasting infrastructure.

Why we exist

Grassroots football can bring a community together, support small businesses, give young people structured work and keep venues safe. The cup is built around those five goals, and Mushin LGA is the pilot.

How it works

1. Compete

20 teams from ten zones play a safe, free, verified competition.

2. Score

Each community is scored on a 100-point viability test.

3. Build

Strong scores unlock a 500-seat pavilion, then a stadium for repeat top performers.

Who is behind it

Hosted by the NYCN, the Mushin CDC and 55 CDAs. Piloted with the South West Development Commission. Technical Secretariat: Best Trade Fulcrum / Foreway Sports and Urban Wear.
